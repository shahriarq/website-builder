# Glossary

Persian originals as they appear in W1 (page) or V1 (auto-captions, so
spelling may be off). Tags as in the rulebook.

| Term | Persian | Meaning in SP2L | Tag | Source |
|------|---------|-----------------|-----|--------|
| SP2L | اس‌پی‌تو‌ال | "Spike – 2 Leg"; the author's own name for the setup, chosen to keep it distinct from his other strategies | `[VERIFIED]` | W1 title, V1 00:24:29–00:25:55 |
| Spike | اسپایک (captions: اسپاک) | Powerful, sudden move of several large candles in one direction, after a range, built on a valid breakout and containing a P-Gap | `[VERIFIED]` | W1-2..4, V1-1, V1-5 |
| P-Gap, pressure gap | پی‌گپ / گپ فشار / گپ قیمتی | Gap between spike candles that marks real buying/selling pressure; the validity condition. Operational test not yet defined | `[VERIFIED]` term, test open (Q13) | W1-3, V1-7 |
| E-Gap, exhaustion gap | ای‌گپ | Gap at the end of a move; a third repetition of the pattern on one move is probably this → skip | `[VERIFIED]` | V1-7, V1-14 |
| Common gap / morning gap | گپ مشترک / گپ صبحگاهی | Other gap types the author distinguishes; not SP2L triggers | `[VERIFIED]` mention only | V1 00:32:05 |
| Breakout | بریک‌اوت | A candle closing beyond the prior candle's high (bullish); valid when the next candle does not overlap it | `[VERIFIED]` | V1-6, V1-8 |
| Follow-through / key bar / signal bar | فالو‌ترو / کی‌بار / سیگنال‌بار | The candle after the breakout that confirms it by closing further and not overlapping; also used as an entry confirmation | `[VERIFIED]` (Q17 for the confirmation role) | V1-6, V1-24 |
| Correction | اصلاح | The pullback after the spike; begins when a candle trades below the previous candle's low (bullish) | `[VERIFIED]` | W1-5, W1-6, V1-19 |
| 1st leg / leg one | لگ اول / لگ وان | The spike itself | `[VERIFIED]` | V1-16 |
| 2nd leg / leg two | لگ دوم / لگ تو | The continuation after the correction, expected equal to leg 1; "activation" = the corrective candle hitting the previous candle's low/high | `[VERIFIED]` | W1-6..9, V1-16 |
| 3rd leg | لگ سوم | A further leg; traded by a different method (weekly report #19), not part of SP2L | `[VERIFIED]` | V1-17 |
| Level / entry level | سطح ورود | The price where the trade is taken: previous candle's low (buy) / high (sell), via limit order | `[VERIFIED]` | W1 §3.3, V1-19..21 |
| Supplementary entry / "2X" | ورود تکمیلی / دو ایکس | Add-on position at 50% of the entry-to-SL distance; earns 3R to TP1 | `[VERIFIED]` | W1-8, V1-27 |
| TP1 / TP2 | تی‌پی یک / تی‌پی دو | Targets; TP1 = 1R default; TP2 further (distance not stated) | `[VERIFIED]` | W1-12/13, V1-29 |
| R, reward | ریوارد / آر | Multiple of the initial risk ("R1", "R2") | `[VERIFIED]` | V1 00:21:46, 00:59:32 |
| Equilibrium price | قیمت تعادلی | The moving average (author uses 60 EMA on M1); wait for price to return to it before the next setup | `[VERIFIED]` | V1-49, V1-50 |
| Round levels | راند لول / اعداد رند | Price levels every 250 / 500 / 1000 points on gold (fast scalper / scalper / swing) | `[VERIFIED]` | V1-45 |
| Clean vs dirty market | تمیز / کثیف | Clean = trending, non-overlapping candles; dirty = overlapping range candles → stay out | `[VERIFIED]` | V1-51 |
| Set and forget | ست اند فورگت | Place the order with SL/TP and do not interfere | `[VERIFIED]` | V1-34 |
| Agnosticism | ندانم‌گرایی | The author's mindset: "I don't know" what comes next; act on the plan | `[VERIFIED]` | V1 00:14:08 |
