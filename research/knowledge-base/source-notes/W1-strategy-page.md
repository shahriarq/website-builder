## Gaps noticed

- The page never defines the **P-Gap** operationally (between which two candles, wick or body). → Q13.
- "کف کندل قبلی" (low of the previous candle) — previous relative to what? The last spike candle, or whichever candle precedes the one that breaks a low? → Q3/Q4.
- "پشت کندلی که اسپایک از آن آغاز شده" — is the SL at that candle's low, or beyond the candle before it? Buffer not stated. → Q5.
- TP2 appears only in the image alt text; its distance is not stated. → Q6.
- Minimum/maximum spike size not stated. → Q1.
- "اصلاح کوتاه" (short correction) — no depth limit beyond the SL. → Q2.
- The chart image (`backcenter.poursamadi.com/uploads/2026/08/...sp2l-compressed-879f5c9a.jpg`) was not supplied. It is the only worked example on the page. → inventory C1.
